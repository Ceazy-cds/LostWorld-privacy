Lost World - Privacy Policy

Developer: Ceazy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Lost World, developed by Ceazy, handles information when you use the game.

Lost World is an adventure and exploration game for Android. The first three levels are free. Additional levels can be unlocked through optional rewarded advertisements or through a one-time in-app purchase.

2. Information We Collect

Lost World does not require account registration. The developer does not directly request your name, address, or telephone number to play.

The game may store gameplay progress, unlocked levels, and purchase entitlement information. Third-party services may automatically process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, diagnostic information, and interaction data as described below.

3. Advertising - Google AdMob

Lost World uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements.

Players may choose to watch a rewarded video to unlock an additional level. Watching these advertisements is voluntary.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from network data, and advertising interactions.

Advertising data processing is governed by Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy .

4. In-App Purchases

Lost World offers an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the complete game and removes advertising.

Payments are processed by Google Play Billing. Ceazy does not receive or store your payment card details.

Google Play may process purchase and transaction information to complete purchases, verify entitlements, and support purchase restoration.

5. Game Progress and Storage

Game progress and level unlocks may be stored locally on your device.

Locally stored progress may be lost if the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Eligible purchases can be restored through Google Play using the purchasing account.

6. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Lost World uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent for advertising-related data processing.

Users may accept, reject, or manage available consent options. Where required, users can revisit their privacy choices within the app.

7. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by Google services used for advertising and in-app purchases. Ceazy does not sell personal information directly to data brokers.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay information can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion policies. For privacy-related questions, contact the developer using the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

Lost World is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

The developer does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, please contact us.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the game. However, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when game features, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
